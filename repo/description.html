Simple LG launcher is a native, quieter home screen for LG webOS TVs, smooth at the TV's full frame rate: a clock, the weather, your day from Google Calendar and one row of apps over your own wallpaper. A fork of Hearth by rudra-avatar.

  • Weather from Open-Meteo, no account: now, an hourly curve and seven days ahead.
  • Calendar: secret iCal links from Google Calendar or any .ics, several at once, with a month view.
  • Frosted-glass cards that go light or dark depending on the picture behind them, as frosted as you like.
  • News and a quote of the day, quick TV settings on the Down key, and your apps in one row: move, hide or uninstall them from the remote.
  • Wallpaper: one picture or a slideshow from USB or the TV's storage.
  • Thirteen languages (machine-translated), following the TV's language, and a switch to look for new versions on GitHub once a day.
  • Pixel shift for OLED screens.

Needs a rooted TV. It can be made the TV's Home from its own settings (Settings → Home screen → How the launcher opens; the Home app way needs webOS 25 or newer). Setup instructions are on the project page.
