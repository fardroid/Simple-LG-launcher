# Test fixtures

- `feeds/`: small feeds written for the tests (RSS 2.0 as Google News writes it, RSS with CDATA, Atom, RSS 1.0, and one in windows-1251). The headlines and outlets are made up.
- `quote/`: pages of Wikiquote (main pages, the Italian quote-of-the-day feed, a few authors' pages) and a short Wikidata reply, as the launcher receives them. Wikiquote's texts are available under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); the authors are listed in each page's history on its Wikiquote.
